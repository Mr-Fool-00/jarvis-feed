### 2. arxiv:2607.00918 "From Personas to Plot" — MAGNET story generation framework `arxiv:2607.00918-personas-to-plot-magnet` · Score: 7/10

**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.00918  
**Why it matters:** MAGNET is a multi-agent narrative generation framework where persona-grounded character agents propose actions against a shared world state and evolving story goals. ATLAS is a companion graph-based hallucination detector that compares scene-level world representations to catch contradictions automatically.

**Results:** 50% fewer hallucinations vs. single-model baseline. 41% less manual annotation burden. Characters maintain consistent voice because each runs its own persona context rather than sharing one monolith context with a coordinator.

**Leo relevance:** Direct hit on the fiction pipeline. The ATLAS hallucination detector is especially interesting — automatically flagging when a scene contradicts established world state is exactly the pain point in long-form generation. This is a design pattern, not just a paper.

**Step 4.5 gate:** Research paper only — no third-party code to install. Read-only.

**Build_worthy:** Not yet — but this is briefing-worthy for design reference.
