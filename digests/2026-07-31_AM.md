### 4. arXiv:2607.21503 — Agentic Context Management: Selective Persistence in Long Agentic Sessions
**Source:** arXiv (July 23, 2026) · **Score:** 6/10

Studies which information actually needs to persist in an agent's context across tool calls and sub-tasks vs. what can be safely dropped. Identifies three persistence categories: (1) goal state, (2) constraint set, (3) action history. Everything else is reconstructable and doesn't need to occupy context. Empirically shows that selective persistence of just these three reduces context exhaustion events by 60% in long-running agentic tasks while maintaining task coherence.

The "everything else is reconstructable" framing is useful for your pipeline's context handoff protocol.
**Build verdict:** INFORMATIONAL — read abstract, apply principle to your CC session context handoff
🔗 https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21503
