# Anthropic "When AI Builds Itself" — 8/10

## What it is
On June 4, Anthropic published a paper about a concept called "recursive self-improvement" — the idea that AI might soon be able to design and train its own, more powerful successor without humans running the show. They also made an unusual public statement: they're calling for a global pause on building the most powerful AI systems, because they're worried development is moving faster than human oversight can keep up with.

The numbers in the paper are the real story: Claude now writes **more than 80% of the code** that ships inside Anthropic itself. Their engineers are shipping **8 times more code per quarter** than they were from 2021-2025. Their internal "Mythos Preview" model (the restricted one, not the public Fable 5) beat human researchers 64% of the time on choosing the next research direction, across 129 hard research moments.

## Why you'd want it
You're already operating at a level where Claude handles the majority of the actual writing in your pipeline. This paper is Anthropic confirming that the same pattern — AI doing 80%+ of the core production work while humans direct — is exactly how their own world-class engineering team is operating right now. You're not doing something unusual. You're doing what's already the norm at the most AI-native company on earth.

Also relevant: Karpathy (who joined Anthropic's pre-training team last month) is working on the exact thing this paper warns about. He's using AI to speed up AI training. The internal tension between calling for a pause and actively building toward RSI is real — but it's less contradictory than it looks once you read the paper's argument.

## Why I think it's worth your attention
The 80% stat validates your approach. The 8× velocity number gives you a benchmark to measure your own pipeline against. And the pause call is a genuine signal from the people building this that things are moving faster than governance can track — which is context you should have as you're deciding how much to depend on these systems for your revenue model.

## What to do
Read the paper. It's Anthropic's Institute content, around 2000 words. Takes 10 minutes. Think about what it means that the people building Claude are using it the same way you are, and that they consider this a meaningful enough moment to call for a global slowdown.
