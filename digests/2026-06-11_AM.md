### 5. AgentOrchestra arXiv 2506.12508 — TEA Protocol for Lifecycle-Aware Multi-Agent Coordination
**Source:** arxiv.org · **Score:** 5/10  
Proposes the Tool-Environment-Agent (TEA) Protocol: agents, tools, AND execution environments are treated as first-class entities with their own lifecycles (spawn, run, checkpoint, terminate). The orchestrator coordinates lifecycle transitions across all three. The insight: most agent frameworks only coordinate agent-agent interactions; this framework also coordinates the state of the environment (files, databases, running processes) that agents share. Useful framing if you're building an orchestrator for parallel chapter-writing agents that touch shared files.
