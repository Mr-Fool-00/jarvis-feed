### 2. Skill Retrieval Augmentation (SRA) — why skill enumeration will break your system at scale
**Source:** arxiv:2604.24594 · **Score:** 7/10
Academic paper formalizing a paradigm that you'll hit in practice: when agent skill corpora grow large, putting them all in context (enumeration) degrades accuracy dramatically. The paper proposes Skill Retrieval Augmentation — dynamically retrieving only the relevant skill(s) per task rather than enumerating everything. They build SRA-Bench, the first benchmark for this pipeline, and show substantial performance improvements from need-aware skill selection.
**Why it matters:** Your current Jarvis skill library has ~20-30 skills. At 100+, enumeration in every context window will start hurting performance — Claude will either miss the right skill or get confused by irrelevant ones. The design decision is: (a) keep skills small and sharp so enumeration stays cheap, OR (b) build a retrieval layer that finds the right skill dynamically. This paper gives you the vocabulary and evidence for that architecture call before you hit the wall. **Build verdict: INFORMATIONAL — design awareness now, retrieval layer is a future build.**
🔗 https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.24594
