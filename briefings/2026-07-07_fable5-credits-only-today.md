## What it is

Anthropic is switching Fable 5 from included-in-Max to credits-only pricing at midnight CDT tonight (July 7 → July 8). After the cutover, Fable 5 costs $10/M input tokens and $50/M output tokens — double what Opus 4.8 costs. This is framed as a capacity constraint, not a policy shift: Anthropic says it intends to restore standard access "as soon as capacity allows" but hasn't given a date.
