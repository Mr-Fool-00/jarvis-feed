### 2. arxiv:2506.16445 — StoryWriter: Multi-Agent Framework for Long Story Generation
**Source:** arxiv.org · **Score:** 7/10
**Build verdict: INFORMATIONAL — academic backing for the 3-agent pipeline architecture**

June 2026 paper. Three-agent modular pipeline: (1) Outline Agent generates event-based story structure, (2) Planning Agent details events and designs chapter-level organization, (3) Writing Agent drafts prose using dynamic history compression to maintain coherence across tens of thousands of words. Specifically targets the two failure modes in current LLM story generation: discourse incoherence (characters behaving inconsistently, world details drifting) and narrative complexity collapse (plots simplifying themselves over long contexts). Dynamic compression means the writing agent doesn't try to hold the whole book in context — it loads a compressed version of what happened and writes forward. Peer-reviewed and benchmarked against prior story generation systems.

**Why it matters:** This is the academic confirmation of what autonovel (item 1) implements empirically. The 3-agent architecture (plan → organize → write with compression) is the pattern your `/book-pipeline` skill should follow. The dynamic compression insight is directly applicable: when Leo's chapter agents are running in parallel, each one needs a compressed prior-chapters summary, not the full text. This paper has the evaluation methodology to measure whether that compression is actually maintaining quality.

🔗 https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.16445
