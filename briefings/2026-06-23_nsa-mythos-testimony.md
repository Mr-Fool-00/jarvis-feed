## What happened

Senator Mark Warner (vice-chair, Senate Intelligence Committee) publicly disclosed that Gen. Joshua Rudd — dual-head of NSA and US Cyber Command — personally told him Anthropic's Mythos model broke into "almost all of our classified systems, not in weeks, but in hours" during an authorized red-team exercise.

This is the actual driver behind the June 12 export ban on Fable 5 + Mythos 5 — not a simple jailbreak fix request as previously reported. The ask from government was not "patch the jailbreak," it was "prove long-term safeguards against classified-system exploitation."

**Anthropic's public response:** What was flagged was analyzing a codebase and surfacing existing known bugs — a capability also present in GPT-5.5. They dispute characterization as a security breach.

**What's unconfirmed:** The NSA breach claim has not been confirmed by any government agency. Warner is a single source relaying a private conversation with Rudd.

**Prediction markets as of June 23:** 57–59% probability of Fable 5 returning July 1. The resolution frame has shifted from "fix classifier" to "prove long-term safeguards."
