# MCP Tunnels + Self-hosted Sandboxes — 7/10

## What it is
Two new security features for Claude Managed Agents announced May 19. MCP Tunnels let agents reach MCP servers inside private networks without exposing them to the public internet. Self-hosted sandboxes (public beta) let tool execution run on your own infrastructure — Cloudflare, Daytona, Modal, or Vercel — while Anthropic handles orchestration.

## Why you'd want it (specific to your stack)
Your Jarvis system runs on your Mac right now, but if you ever move to managed agents (which Dreaming + Outcomes would enable), these features mean your MCP servers stay private and tool execution stays on infrastructure you control. The Cloudflare angle is especially relevant since your Jarvis Worker already runs on Cloudflare.

## Why I think it's worth your attention
Future-proofing. Not actionable today, but when managed agents mature enough for your writing pipeline, this is the security layer that makes it viable.

## What to do
Read the announcement for awareness. No action needed until you're ready to move Jarvis to managed agents.
