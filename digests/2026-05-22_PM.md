### 3. earezki: 5-Agent Book Writing System with DAG Dependencies
**Source:** earezki.com · **Score:** 7/10

Build verdict: INFORMATIONAL — your pipeline is already more sophisticated; DAG pattern is interesting but linear processing fits your sequential-chapter workflow.
