**Source:** github:mgechev/skillgrade · **Score:** 7/10

The PATTERN, not the specific library, is the takeaway — worth adapting into Jarvis's orchestration architecture.
🔗 https://developers.googleblog.com/announcing-genkit-middleware-intercept-extend-and-harden-your-agentic-apps/
