### 1. Sonnet 5 Can Cost *More* Than Opus 4.8 in Complex Multi-Step Pipelines
**Source:** MindStudio Blog · **Score:** 7/10 · **build_worthy:** ✅ YES

Sonnet 5's new tokenizer uses ~30% more tokens than Sonnet 4.6 for the same content. In multi-step agentic pipelines, this compounds: each step's verbose output becomes the next step's inflated input, and you pay the overrun twice per hop. MindStudio ran benchmarks showing multi-step agentic jobs on Sonnet 5 exceeding the same jobs on Opus 4.8 in total token cost. Their recs: trim system prompts by 30%, add explicit length constraints to every step, and route long-chain agentic work to Opus 4.8.

**Why it matters:** Your writing pipeline is exactly the multi-step agentic case they're describing — chapter generation → judge → fixer → peer review is 4+ hops, each compounding the overinflation. On Max plan you don't pay per token, but you burn weekly capacity faster and may trigger rate limits sooner than you expect. Worth auditing your system prompt lengths against the new tokenizer, and testing whether routing your 4-hop fixers to Opus 4.8 extends your weekly run budget.
